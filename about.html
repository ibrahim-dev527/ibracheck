Ibrahim Mohammed Lotsu

Accounting Student & Education Innovator

Hi! I'm Ibrahim Mohammed Lotsu, a passionate accounting student in the Faculty of Business, Accounting Department. I created IbraCheck out of a desire to help fellow students master complex accounting concepts through interactive, engaging learning experiences.

Throughout my academic journey, I noticed that traditional study methods weren't always effective for everyone. That's when I decided to combine technology with education to create a platform that makes learning finance and accounting both fun and effective.
